About the model

One Poisson model is fitted across the Premier League, LaLiga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, the Champions League and the Europa League together. Each club is rated relative to its league, and the leagues are put on one scale by the games between them. Recent results count more: a result from a year ago counts half. Clubs from other leagues are rated from their European results.

Knockout ties are worked out across both legs, including extra time and penalties. Played matchdays show what the model predicted the morning before, using only results known then. Markets settle on the 90-minute score.

Corners use the same structure, fitted on corner counts from Champions League and Premier League games. Each fixture gets one goals tip and one corners tip, chosen by the same rules as the Premier League page.

Backtested on every game from the league phase onward, each predicted the morning before using only earlier results: the corners tip landed 77% of the time in 2025/26 and 79% in 2024/25, and the goals tip 74% and 71%. In both seasons, every corner line the model tipped landed more often than tipping that line on every game. It is a model, not a tip service.
